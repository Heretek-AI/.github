# Security policy

This is the default policy for Heretek-AI repositories. A repository's own
`SECURITY.md` overrides it and may add scope and timelines.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately, not in a public issue.

1. On the affected repository, open the **Security** tab and choose
   **Report a vulnerability**.
2. If that option is absent, open an issue titled "Security contact request"
   with no technical detail. A maintainer will open a private advisory and
   invite you.

Include the affected repository, the commit or version, reproduction steps or
a proof of concept, and the impact you expect.

## What to expect

A maintainer acknowledges the report and keeps the advisory private until a
fix ships. Reporters are credited unless they ask to stay anonymous.

Test against your own deployment, never against infrastructure you do not own.
